Meet Pip. A little raccoon with a big eye for treasure — and a little help from you.

Pick two objects. Pip steals everything that shares their color, shape, or both. The clever part? Leaving the right things for your next grab.

See how to play

Coming soon for iPhone · iOS 17 or later

A little look. A clever grab.

  1. Pick two. Choose two unprotected objects with a color or shape in common. If they share both, both must match.
  2. Check the preview. See exactly what Pip will collect, including your two examples. Protected objects stay safe: a matching one blocks the grab.
  3. Make your move. Clear the targets within your grabs. Plan what you leave behind — those objects could unlock your next move.

50 heists and a gentle first lesson. No timers. Free undo, hints, and restart give every new idea room to breathe.

Make Pip your own.

Earn Rose and Midnight outfits as you play, or choose optional outfits in the shop. A permanent No Ads purchase is also available in the game. Purchases are optional; there are no subscriptions.

The game works offline. Ads and App Store purchases use an internet connection. Ads can appear at completion breaks starting with Heist 3; the tutorial and first two heists have no ads.
