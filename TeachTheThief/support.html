A little help for your heist.

For help with Teach the Thief, email vinicioscunha1@gmail.com.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the heist number if relevant. A screenshot and the steps that led to the problem help. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

How do I make a grab?

Pick two different, unprotected objects. They must share a color, shape, or both. Pip collects every matching object, including the two you picked. If both traits match, only objects sharing both are collected. A protected match blocks the grab without spending one.

Stuck on a puzzle?

Use Hint to find a next move, Undo to take back a grab, or restart the heist. These options are free. Think about which examples you will need after the current grab.

Restore a purchase

Open the in-game shop and choose Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. A connection is required to check purchases. No Ads and the paid outfits are permanent, nonconsumable purchases.

If a purchase is awaiting approval, you can keep playing. If it was declined or expired, restore purchases to recheck and then try again. For billing or refund requests, visit Apple’s purchase support.

Saved progress

Progress and game settings are stored on your device. There is no game account or developer-operated cloud save service. Deleting the app can remove your progress; restoring purchases restores eligible purchased items, not level progress.

Ads and privacy choices

Advertising is optional. The game asks for Apple’s tracking permission before enabling Google’s advertising and consent services. If you decline, permission is restricted, or you have not granted permission, you can keep playing with no ads. Verified No Ads owners are not asked for this permission by the game.

You can change tracking permission in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Regional ad privacy choices are separate from Apple’s permission.

When available for your region, open Settings and choose Ad privacy choices to revisit your choices. No Ads removes game advertising when ownership is verified. If a consent screen or ad cannot load, you can keep playing.

Read the privacy policy for details about local saves, Apple purchases, and third-party advertising services.
